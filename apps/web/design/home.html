Inspections

Captured on the landing, not typed up later

Routine, move-in and move-out use one format. Marks and photographs are held on the phone and sync when the signal comes back — a write never reports success it cannot prove.

  1. Chimney filters clogged — tenant-borne, added to the next invoice
  2. Window latch broken — owner-borne, job raised on the spot
  3. Balcony railing loose — flagged as a safety item, escalated same day
  4. Seepage at the shower trap — quoted, owner approved in a tap

The 6th of the month

What the owner actually sees

One flat, one month, entry by entry — closing at nothing unexplained. This is the statement that stops the phone call, and it is the same ledger your accountant, your auditor and your owner are all reading.
